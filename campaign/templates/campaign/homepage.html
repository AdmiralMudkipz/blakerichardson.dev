Use the Grants, Unleash Solar Power, Transparent and Responsive Government.

Our township is leaving millions in grants on the table. These grants can be used for paths, crosswalks, and safety improvements around schools and playgrounds. I will push to take advantage of all these grants to improve our town. Using state money you've already paid for, instead of costing us on the local level.

We must make it easier for everyone to build solar. Our township has backwards, decade old solar laws that make building solar a time consuming headache. Solar is now the most affordable source of electricity, in a world where AI is spiking our utility costs, we deserve to have Power over our own Power.

Our township desperately needs transparency. Our meetings are no longer viewable online which prevents many working people from staying informed. I will ensure that township meetings are available to the public online and archived, so busy working people can have a voice in our future as well.

If we tried to build Main Street today, it would be illegal. New development lately has been completely different from what made our Harrison great. I want our town to grow with the same charm and character that founded it, and we can start by relegalizing traditional forms of development that made our town what it is today.
