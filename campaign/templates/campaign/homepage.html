Use the Grants, Unleash Solar Power, Transparent and Responsive Government.

Our township is leaving millions in grants on the table. I will apply for these grants to improve our town with state money you've already paid for, instead of costing us on the local level.

We must make it possible for everyone to build solar. Our township has backwards, decade old solar laws that make building solar a time consuming headache. Electricity prices are higher than ever, and we deserve to have Power over our own Power.

Our township desperately needs transparency. I will ensure that township meetings are available to the public online and archived, so busy working people can have a voice in our future as well.

If we tried to build Main Street today, it would be illegal. New development lately has been completely different from what made our Harrison great. I want our town to grow with the same charm and character that founded it, and we can start by relegalizing traditional forms of development that made our town what it is today.
