Issue Brief

Cheap Energy

A local policy roadmap for more affordable and resilient power generation.

Issue & Policy: Permitting Reform / Streamline Bureaucracy

Automate Solar Permitting (SolarApp+)

Do solar permitting via SolarApp+ utilizing the NJ Smart Solar Permitting Law (A5264).

Effects: Effectively automates solar permitting, saving both the township and residents thousands a year while streamlining permitting and construction to get desperately needed solar savings rapidly during our ongoing energy crisis.

Issue & Policy: Energy Production / Cut Red-Tape

End Township Review of Street-Facing Roof Solar

Repeal § 225-132D(4)(f), § 110-1.1J: Remove mandatory township review for ROW facing roof solar.

Effects: Removes unnecessary red tape around installing roof solar. This law needlessly involves the township in something inconsequential and makes solar installation more complicated for homeowners for no tangible benefit.

Issue & Policy: Energy Production / Cut Red-Tape

Allow Both Ground and Rooftop Solar

Repeal § 225-132D(3): Allow a property to have both ground and rooftop solar.

Effects: This is a completely arbitrary rule with no benefit. If a property can use more solar than it has roof space they ought to be able to build that much solar generation by right.

Issue & Policy: Energy Production / Community Solar Legalization

Legalize Community Solar, Commercial Solar Coverage, and Parking Canopy Solar

Legalize community solar and allow companies to cover their roofs and excess land with solar panels, including solar panel canopies over parking lots, by right.

Condition: projects must include ample battery storage and pass the power company’s interconnection and grid-reliability vetting requirements.

Effects: Keeps township government out of unnecessary project-level energy decisions while preserving grid safety through utility vetting. Expands local generation, lowers long-term energy costs, and increases resilient distributed power capacity.

Issue & Policy: Residential Energy Access / Plug-In Solar

Legalize Plug-In Solar

Legalize plug-in solar systems for homes and renters, provided installations are implemented according to the power company’s safety and interconnection standards.

Effects: Expands access to lower-cost solar for households that cannot install full rooftop systems, while maintaining grid and fire safety through utility safety requirements.

Issue & Policy: Energy Production / Cut Red-Tape

Allow Solar Power Plants

Repeal § 225-132D(1): Allow properties to have solar installations in which the principle use is the production of electricity via solar.

Effects: Unbans solar power plants.

Issue & Policy: Energy Production / Cut Red-Tape

Allow Ground Solar on Small Lots

Repeal § 225-132D(5)(b): Allow ground solar on <1 acre properties.

Effects: The feasibility of a solar installation should be decided by the property owner. This opens up ground solar opportunities for residents with smaller plots.

Issue & Policy: Energy Production / Cut Red-Tape

Shrink Solar Panel Setbacks to 10 Feet

Modify § 225-132D(6)(a): Reduce solar panel setback rule to the same as sheds, 10 feet from the property boundary.

Effects: Enables solar installations in areas with less land to work with. No need for excessively large setbacks.
