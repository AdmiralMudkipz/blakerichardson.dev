Campaign FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick, straight answers, no spin.

What are the campaign's top priorities?

Four things: going after the grants our township ignores, fixing our outdated solar rules, putting meetings online for good, and relegalizing the kind of Main Street development that built this town in the first place.

Where can I read policy details?

Head to the Issues page for the overview, then click into Cut Red Tape, Cheap Energy, or Safe Streets for the specific changes I want to make.

Is this website collecting contact form submissions yet?

Not yet, for now, just email or call me directly and I'll get back to you myself.

How can I share local concerns?

Use the Contact page. Tell me what neighborhood you're in and what's going on, and I'll look into it.
