Campaign FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the campaign platform and where to find policy details.

What are the campaign's top priorities?

The platform focuses on three core areas: cutting red tape, lowering long-term energy costs, and improving street safety through practical infrastructure changes.

Where can I read policy details?

Visit the Issues page for a full overview, then open each issue brief for detailed proposals and expected impacts.

Is this website collecting contact form submissions yet?

Not yet. The current contact page is static and provides direct communication channels. A full submission workflow can be added in a later phase.

How can I share local concerns?

Use the Contact page to send campaign questions and neighborhood-specific concerns, including location context when relevant.
