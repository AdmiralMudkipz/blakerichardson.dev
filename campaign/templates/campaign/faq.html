Campaign FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Blake Richardson?

I'm a candidate for Harrison Township Committee in Gloucester County, New Jersey. I grew up in Mullica Hill, attended Clearview, and graduated from Rowan University with a Bachelor's in Computer Science and a Minor in German.

When is the election and what column is Blake Richardson in?

The election is Tuesday, November 3. I'm in Column A. Confirm early voting, mail-in ballot, and polling place details with the Gloucester County Clerk.

What are the campaign's top priorities?

I want to make government simple, fair, and fast. That means maximizing our state grants for safer streets, fixing our outdated solar rules, making township committee meetings available online live and as recordings, and relegalizing the kind of Main Street style development that built this town in the first place.

How would Blake Richardson use grants in Harrison Township?

I'd use state and regional grants such as NJDOT Safe Routes to School and bike path programs to build safe connections between neighborhoods, schools, transit, and Main Street. That means less school drop-off traffic and less strain on the bus system, without a new local tax burden.

Where can I read policy details?

Head to the Issues page for the overview, then click into Cut Red Tape, Cheap Energy, or Safe Streets for the specific changes I want to make.
