About Me

Blake Richardson

My name is Blake Richardson, I grew up in Mullica Hill and have lived here for nearly my entire life. I graduated with a Bachelor's in Computer Science and a Minor in German from Rowan University. Throughout my education I've gain an interest in economics, urban planning, and local policy. Curiosity lead me to researching our own local policies here in Harrison Township. I found a lot of very strange and outdated regulations that have real negative impacts on our community. In light of this, and the national economic environment we find ourselves in, I decided to run for office, to try to make life a bit easier for everyone.

I care deeply about protecting the character of our town while making local government work better for regular people.
