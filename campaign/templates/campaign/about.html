About The Candidate

Blake Richardson

Blake Richardson is running for Township Committee to deliver practical, local policy that residents can feel in everyday life: lower costs, safer streets, and a local government that works faster and communicates clearly.

This campaign is grounded in implementation. The focus is on specific policy changes, transparent decision-making, and measurable outcomes for families, commuters, small businesses, and students.

Rather than broad slogans, Blake's approach is to publish clear policy proposals, explain expected impacts, and keep residents informed as work moves from planning to execution.

Campaign Priorities

  • Modernize outdated rules that block housing and small business opportunity.
  • Expand practical energy options that lower long-term household costs.
  • Improve school-area and Main Street safety through funded street redesign.
  • Increase transparency and access to local government information.
